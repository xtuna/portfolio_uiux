About This Piece

A homepage design for a travel and tour booking website, built around a bold hero search module, destination highlight cards, social proof through guest reviews, and a regional trip carousel. The goal was a warm, adventurous visual identity that still reads as trustworthy enough to book a trip through.

Design Notes

Color & Typography

A near-black base lets a single accent orange carry all primary actions, ratings, and highlight text — with white and neutral gray reserved for content cards, so the color always signals something worth noticing.

Style Direction

Warm and adventurous rather than corporate — large destination photography, rounded cards, and a dark canvas that lets the imagery and orange accent do most of the visual work.
