Nike Concept — E-Commerce Product Page UI Design

A dark, editorial-style product page concept for Nike sneakers — one layout reused across five shoe models, each with its own colorway switcher and a vivid gradient splash behind the product shot.

About This Piece

An unofficial fan concept exploring what a Nike product page could look like with a bolder, more editorial art direction — floating product photography set against a dynamic color-splash gradient, a compact colorway switcher, and a side carousel for browsing between models without leaving the page.

E-Commerce Dark UI Product Photography Desktop

This is an unofficial concept design created for portfolio/practice purposes, using Nike's name and product photography for reference. It is not affiliated with or endorsed by Nike, Inc.

Design Notes

Color & Typography

A near-black canvas everywhere means the gradient splash and product photography are the only real color in the frame, so attention goes straight to the shoe rather than the interface around it.

Style Direction

Bold and editorial rather than a standard retail grid — one hero product per page, a compact colorway switcher, and a right-hand carousel with arrow navigation for browsing between models without a full page reload.
