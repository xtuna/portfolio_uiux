EV Landing Page: "Model 4x"

A landing page concept for an electric vehicle brand — a confident, photography-led hero showcasing the "Model 4x," paired with bold spec callouts and a clear path to pricing.

About This Piece

A single-page hero design for an EV brand site, modeled after the category's now-familiar visual language — minimal navigation, one dramatic product shot, and specs presented as headline-sized numbers rather than a spec sheet. The goal was to make the car itself carry the design, with UI elements staying quiet and out of the way.

Typography

Helvetica Neue Cyr carries the entire page — one family, weight changes doing all the work, from the oversized "Model 4x" wordmark down to spec labels and fine print.

Design Notes

Color & Typography

A near-white canvas keeps the product photo as the focal point; amber is reserved for the one primary action ("Get a Quote") so it never competes with the car itself.

Style Direction

Confident and restrained — big type, generous whitespace, and a spec presentation that borrows from the automotive category's now-standard "numbers as headlines" pattern.
