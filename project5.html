About This Piece

A donut shop landing page designed as one reusable layout scaffold — nav, hero illustration, headline, and a flavor-picker row — restyled four times to match four different donut flavors. The exercise was about proving the layout holds up under different color moods rather than designing four separate pages from scratch.

Typography

An elegant script carries the "Donuts" wordmark for warmth and craft-bakery character, paired with a plain, highly legible sans-serif for the body copy and navigation — so charm never comes at the cost of readability.

Design Notes

Color & Typography

Every variant reuses the same white content card, dark text, and layout grid — only the background gradient, blob shape, and flavor accent icons change, keeping the system easy to extend to new flavors later.

Style Direction

Playful and appetizing, with a soft organic blob shape behind each donut illustration to keep the pages feeling handcrafted rather than templated, despite sharing one underlying layout.
