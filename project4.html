About This Piece

A full ordering flow for "Tomato," a fictional burger delivery app — from an animated-feeling splash screen through menu browsing, product detail with a portion and spice-level selector, and a clean checkout summary. The goal was an appetite-driving visual style that still keeps ordering fast and unambiguous.

Typography

A cursive script carries the "Tomato" logotype on the splash screens for personality, while a clean grotesque sans-serif handles everything functional — menu items, prices, and buttons — so readability never competes with brand flair.

Design Notes

Color & Typography

A soft pink stripe pattern keeps every screen visually tied together without competing with the food photography, while red is reserved for price, ratings, and the primary "Order Now" action.

Style Direction

Warm and appetite-driving rather than purely functional — large, unobstructed food photography, rounded cards, and a script logotype that gives the brand personality without slowing down ordering.
